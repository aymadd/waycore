# Troubleshooting

> Common issues and how to resolve them

## Quick Fixes

Before diving into specific issues, try these general fixes:

1. **Restart the app**: Close and reopen the problematic app
2. **Restart the device**: Power off, wait 10 seconds, power on
3. **Check battery**: Low battery can cause unexpected behavior
4. **Check storage**: Full storage can prevent apps from working

## Device Issues

### Device won't turn on
**Symptoms**: No response to power button

**Solutions**:
1. Connect to charger for 15 minutes
2. Try holding power button for 10+ seconds
3. Check charging cable and port
4. If still unresponsive, contact support

### Device is very slow
**Symptoms**: Apps take a long time to respond

**Solutions**:
1. Close unused apps
2. Restart the device
3. Check storage space (Settings → Info)
4. Let device cool if hot

### Screen is unresponsive
**Symptoms**: Touch doesn't register, or registers incorrectly

**Solutions**:
1. Clean the screen
2. Remove any screen protector and test
3. Restart the device
4. Check for physical damage

### Device overheating
**Symptoms**: Device feels hot, performance drops

**Solutions**:
1. Move to shade or cooler location
2. Close GPS-intensive apps
3. Reduce screen brightness
4. Let device cool before continuing

## Battery Issues

### Battery draining quickly
**Symptoms**: Battery depletes faster than expected

**Solutions**:
1. Reduce screen brightness
2. Shorter auto-sleep time (Settings → General)
3. Disable unused radios (Settings → Mesh)
4. Close apps using GPS continuously
5. Check for runaway apps using battery

### Device won't charge
**Symptoms**: No charging indicator when plugged in

**Solutions**:
1. Try a different USB-C cable
2. Try a different power source
3. Check USB-C port for debris
4. Clean port gently with compressed air
5. Restart device and try again

### Battery percentage incorrect
**Symptoms**: Battery shows wrong level or jumps around

**Solutions**:
1. Let battery drain to 5% then fully charge
2. Restart device
3. If persists, battery may need replacement

## GPS Issues

### No GPS fix
**Symptoms**: "No GPS" or coordinates not updating

**Solutions**:
1. Move outdoors with clear sky view
2. Wait 1-2 minutes for satellite acquisition
3. Avoid dense tree cover or canyons
4. Restart the GPS-using app
5. Check GPS status in Settings → Sensors

### GPS accuracy poor
**Symptoms**: Position jumps around or is offset

**Solutions**:
1. Move to open area
2. Wait for more satellites (check accuracy reading)
3. Urban areas have more interference
4. Accept that some locations have poor GPS

### GPS draining battery
**Solutions**:
1. Close apps using GPS when not needed
2. GPS is power-intensive by design
3. Use compass for heading when position not needed

## Compass Issues

### Compass pointing wrong direction
**Symptoms**: North is not where it should be

**Solutions**:
1. Calibrate compass (Settings → Sensors → Calibrate)
2. Move away from metal objects
3. Remove nearby magnets or electronics
4. Hold device flat and level

### Compass needle spinning
**Symptoms**: Heading jumps around erratically

**Solutions**:
1. Calibrate immediately
2. Strong interference nearby — move location
3. Check for magnets in phone case or accessories

## AI Issues

### AI responses are slow
**Symptoms**: Takes 10+ seconds for simple questions

**Solutions**:
1. Normal for complex questions
2. Keep device cool
3. Close other apps
4. Restart the AI app

### "Model not loaded" error
**Symptoms**: AI won't respond, shows model error

**Solutions**:
1. Wait 30 seconds for model to load
2. Restart the AI app
3. Restart the device
4. Check available storage (needs ~2GB free)

### AI gives irrelevant answers
**Symptoms**: Responses don't match the question

**Solutions**:
1. Rephrase question more specifically
2. Ask one question at a time
3. Include context in your question
4. Clear conversation and start fresh

### Image analysis not working
**Symptoms**: AI doesn't respond to photo questions

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure photo is clear and well-lit
2. Try a different photo
3. Check camera permissions
4. Restart the AI app

## Meshtastic Issues

### Radio not detected
**Symptoms**: "No radio" or connection error

**Solutions**:
1. Check physical connection
2. Go to Settings → Mesh → Reconnect
3. Restart the device
4. Verify radio hardware is compatible

### No nodes visible
**Symptoms**: Empty node list

**Solutions**:
1. Other users may be out of range
2. Check radio is enabled (Settings → Mesh)
3. Wait 2-3 minutes for discovery
4. Move to higher ground for better range

### Messages not sending
**Symptoms**: Messages stuck, not delivered

**Solutions**:
1. Check radio connection
2. Verify at least one node is visible
3. May be out of range — move to better location
4. Check radio power settings

## App-Specific Issues

### Camera won't open
**Solutions**:
1. Close and reopen camera app
2. Check if another app is using camera
3. Restart device

### Notes not saving
**Solutions**:
1. Wait for auto-save (few seconds)
2. Check storage space
3. Restart the app

### Gallery not showing photos
**Solutions**:
1. Pull down to refresh
2. New photos may take a moment to appear
3. Restart the app

## When to Contact Support

Contact support if:
- Device has physical damage
- Problems persist after all troubleshooting
- You see error codes or crashes
- Hardware appears defective

**Before contacting support**:
1. Note your device model and software version
2. Describe the issue clearly
3. List troubleshooting steps you've tried
4. Export logs if possible (Settings → Advanced)
