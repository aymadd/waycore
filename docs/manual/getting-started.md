# Getting Started with Waycore

> Your first steps with the Waycore field computer

## What is Waycore?

Waycore is a portable, rugged field computer designed for outdoor use. It combines navigation, communication, and AI-powered assistance in a single device that works completely offline.

## First Boot

When you power on your Waycore device for the first time:

1. **Power Button**: Press and hold the power button for 2 seconds
2. **Boot Screen**: Wait for the Waycore logo to appear (about 10 seconds)
3. **Home Screen**: You'll see the main grid of apps

## Home Screen Overview

The home screen displays your most-used apps in a grid layout:

| Icon | App | Description |
|------|-----|-------------|
| 🤖 | AI | Ask questions, get sensor data |
| 🧭 | Compass | View heading and GPS coordinates |
| 📻 | Meshtastic | Send mesh messages |
| 📷 | Camera | Take photos |
| 📝 | Notes | Quick notes and reminders |
| ⚙️ | Settings | Device configuration |

**Tip**: Tap any app icon to open it. Swipe down from the top to return home.

## Status Bar

The top of the screen shows important information:

- **Time**: Current time in 24-hour format
- **Battery**: Battery level and charging status
- **Signal**: Mesh radio signal strength (if connected)

## Basic Navigation

### Opening Apps
Tap an app icon to open it.

### Going Back
- Tap the **←** button in the top-left corner
- Or swipe from the left edge of the screen

### Returning Home
- Tap the **⌂** home icon
- Or swipe down from the top edge

## Essential First Steps

### 1. Check Your Battery
Open **Settings → Info** to see your battery level. Charge if below 20%.

### 2. Configure Sensors
Go to **Settings → Sensors** to verify GPS and compass are working.

### 3. Try the AI Assistant
Open the **AI** app and ask "What's the temperature?" to test sensor integration.

### 4. Set Up Mesh (Optional)
If you have a Meshtastic radio, go to **Settings → Mesh** to configure it.

## Power Management

### Sleep Mode
The screen dims after 30 seconds of inactivity to save power.

### Power Off
Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds, then tap "Power Off".

### Charging
Connect the USB-C cable to charge. A full charge takes approximately 2-3 hours.

## Offline Operation

Waycore is designed to work without internet:

- ✅ AI assistant works offline
- ✅ Maps work with cached/downloaded tiles
- ✅ Mesh messaging works via radio
- ✅ All sensors work offline
- ❌ Weather forecasts require internet
- ❌ Map tile downloads require internet
