# Compass

> Digital compass with heading, GPS coordinates, and elevation

## Overview

The Compass app displays your current heading along with GPS position and elevation data. It's designed for quick reference during navigation.

## Getting Started

1. From the Home screen, tap **Compass** (🧭)
2. Hold the device flat and level
3. The compass needle points to magnetic north

## Display Elements

### Compass Rose
The main compass display shows:
- **Red needle**: Points to magnetic north
- **Degree reading**: Current heading (0-360°)
- **Cardinal direction**: N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW

### GPS Coordinates
Below the compass:
- **Latitude**: Your north/south position
- **Longitude**: Your east/west position
- **Accuracy**: GPS precision in meters

### Elevation
- **Altitude**: Height above sea level in meters and feet
- **Source**: GPS or barometric altimeter

## Using the Compass

### Basic Navigation
1. Point the top of the device in your direction of travel
2. Read the heading at the top of the display
3. The direction you're facing is shown in large text

### Following a Bearing
1. Note your target bearing (e.g., 270° for due West)
2. Rotate until the compass shows that heading
3. Walk in the direction the device is pointing

### GPS Coordinates
Tap the coordinates to:
- Copy to clipboard
- Open in Maps app
- Save as a waypoint

## Calibration

If the compass seems inaccurate:

1. Tap **Calibrate** in the menu
2. Hold the device and rotate it in a figure-8 pattern
3. Continue for 10-15 seconds
4. "Calibrated" message appears when done

**When to calibrate:**
- After traveling long distances
- Near large metal structures
- If readings seem erratic

## Accuracy Tips

### For Best Compass Accuracy
- Hold device flat and level
- Keep away from metal objects
- Avoid magnets and electronics
- Calibrate regularly

### For Best GPS Accuracy
- Be outdoors with clear sky view
- Wait for GPS fix (may take 1-2 minutes)
- Stay stationary for accurate reading

## Troubleshooting

### Compass spins erratically
- Calibrate the compass
- Move away from metal/electronics
- Check for nearby magnets

### "No GPS Fix" message
- Move outdoors with clear sky view
- Wait 1-2 minutes for satellite lock
- Check Settings → Sensors for GPS status

### Elevation seems wrong
- GPS elevation can be off by 10-30 meters
- For precise altitude, use Settings → Sensors → Altimeter Calibration
- Set a known elevation for your location

### Coordinates not updating
- Check GPS status in Settings → Sensors
- Walk a short distance to trigger update
- Restart app if stuck
