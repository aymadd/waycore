# Gallery

> View, manage, and share your photos

## Overview

The Gallery displays all photos taken with the Camera app. You can view, delete, and use photos with the AI assistant.

## Getting Started

1. From Home, tap **Gallery** (🖼️)
2. Browse your photos in a grid view
3. Tap any photo to view full-screen

## Viewing Photos

### Grid View
Photos are displayed in a grid, newest first. Scroll to browse all photos.

### Full-Screen View
Tap a photo to see it full-screen:
- **Swipe left/right**: View next/previous photo
- **Pinch**: Zoom in/out
- **Double-tap**: Toggle zoom
- **Tap screen**: Show/hide controls

### Photo Details
In full-screen view, tap **ℹ️** to see:
- Date and time taken
- File size
- Camera used (front/rear)

## Managing Photos

### Deleting Photos
In full-screen view:
1. Tap **🗑️ Delete**
2. Confirm deletion

⚠️ Deleted photos cannot be recovered.

### Bulk Delete
Currently not supported. Delete photos one at a time.

## Using Photos

### With AI Assistant
To analyze a photo:
1. Open the **AI** app
2. Tap **🖼️** to open gallery picker
3. Select your photo
4. Ask your question

### Sharing (Future)
Photo sharing via Meshtastic is planned for a future update.

## Storage Management

Photos use device storage. To manage space:

1. Go to **Settings → Info**
2. Check storage usage
3. Delete unneeded photos from Gallery

### Storage Tips
- Delete duplicate or blurry photos
- Keep only essential documentation
- Transfer important photos to backup when connected

## Troubleshooting

### Photos not appearing
- New photos may take a moment to appear
- Pull down to refresh the gallery
- Check Camera app is saving correctly

### Gallery loads slowly
- Large number of photos affects performance
- Consider deleting unneeded photos
- Restart app if very slow

### Cannot delete photo
- Ensure you're in full-screen view
- Tap the delete button, not just swipe
- Restart app if button not responsive

### Photo appears corrupted
- File may have been damaged
- Delete the corrupted photo
- Ensure adequate storage when taking new photos
