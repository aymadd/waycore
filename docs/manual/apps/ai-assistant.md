# AI Assistant

> Your on-device AI companion for questions, sensor data, and image analysis

## Overview

The AI Assistant is a conversational interface that runs completely on your device. It can:

- Answer questions about outdoor skills, survival, and navigation
- Read your device's sensor data (temperature, GPS, altitude, compass)
- Analyze images to help identify plants, animals, or terrain
- Search your notes and provide contextual help

**Key Feature**: All processing happens on-device — no internet required.

---

## ⚠️ Important Safety Information

**Please read before using the AI Assistant.**

The AI Assistant is a helpful tool, but it has significant limitations:

### The AI Can Be Wrong

- AI-generated information may be **incorrect, incomplete, or outdated**
- The AI cannot see your actual environment or assess real conditions
- Always **independently verify** important information before acting on it

### Not Professional Advice

The AI is **NOT** a substitute for:
- **Medical professionals** — Call emergency services for injuries/illness
- **Survival training** — Take proper wilderness courses
- **Expert identification** — Consult botanists, mycologists, or biologists
- **Professional navigation** — Use proper maps, compass, and GPS devices

### Plant Identification Warning

⚠️ **NEVER eat plants, mushrooms, or berries based solely on AI identification.**

Many poisonous species look nearly identical to edible ones. Misidentification can be **fatal**. Always:
- Consult multiple expert field guides
- Verify with local experts
- When in doubt, do not consume

### Survival Situations

In genuine emergencies:
1. **Use the SOS feature** to contact emergency services
2. Follow established survival protocols
3. Prioritize actual safety over AI suggestions
4. Stay calm and signal for rescue

For complete terms and conditions, see [AI Disclaimer](/docs/legal/ai-disclaimer.md).

## Getting Started

1. From the Home screen, tap **AI** (🤖)
2. Type your question in the text box at the bottom
3. Tap **Send** or press Enter
4. Wait for the response (typically 2-10 seconds)

**First Use**: You'll be asked to acknowledge the safety disclaimer before using the AI Assistant.

## Features

### Text Chat

Ask questions in natural language. The AI works best with specific, clear questions.

**Good examples:**
- "What's my current altitude?"
- "How do I start a fire without matches?"
- "What's the best way to find water in a desert?"
- "How do I read a topographic map?"

**Less effective:**
- Single words like "fire" or "water"
- Very long, multi-part questions

### Sensor Queries

Ask about your current conditions — the AI reads your device sensors:

| Question | Sensor Used |
|----------|-------------|
| "What's the temperature?" | Temperature sensor |
| "Where am I?" | GPS |
| "What direction am I facing?" | Compass |
| "What's my elevation?" | Altimeter |
| "How's my battery?" | Power monitor |
| "What time is it?" | System clock |

### Image Analysis

Attach an image to get AI assistance:

1. Tap the **📷** camera button to take a photo
2. Or tap **🖼️** to select from your gallery
3. Add a question like "What plant is this?"
4. Tap Send

**Use cases:**
- Plant identification (always verify independently!)
- Animal track identification
- Terrain analysis
- Reading trail signs

⚠️ **Reminder**: Image-based plant identification is especially unreliable. Many dangerous species closely resemble safe ones in photos.

### Conversation History

Your conversations are saved automatically:

- **📜 History**: Tap to view past conversations
- **➕ New**: Start a fresh conversation
- **🗑️ Clear**: Delete current conversation

Conversations are stored locally on your device and never uploaded.

### Safety Warnings

The AI automatically adds safety warnings when your question touches on sensitive topics:

| Topic | Warning Shown |
|-------|---------------|
| Plant/mushroom identification | ⚠️ Never consume based on AI |
| Medical/first aid questions | ⚠️ Seek professional medical help |
| Navigation/being lost | ⚠️ Use proper navigation tools |
| Survival situations | ⚠️ Use SOS, follow protocols |

These warnings appear at the top of relevant responses to remind you of the AI's limitations.

## Tips for Better Results

### Be Specific
Instead of "Tell me about fire," ask "How do I start a fire in wet conditions using a ferro rod?"

### Include Context
Mention your situation: "I'm in a pine forest at 8000 feet elevation. What wood should I use for fire?"

### One Question at a Time
The AI handles single questions better than multi-part queries.

### Use Follow-ups
After getting an answer, ask follow-up questions for more detail.

### Verify Critical Information
For anything affecting your safety:
1. Cross-reference with other sources
2. Use your own judgment
3. When in doubt, choose the safer option

## Response Time

Response times vary based on question complexity:

| Query Type | Typical Time |
|------------|--------------|
| Sensor data | 1-2 seconds |
| Simple question | 3-5 seconds |
| Complex question | 5-10 seconds |
| Image analysis | 8-15 seconds |

Keep the device cool — high temperatures can slow processing.

## Troubleshooting

### AI responses are slow
- Normal for complex questions
- Keep device out of direct sunlight
- Close other apps to free memory

### "Model not loaded" error
The AI model may need to initialize on first use:
1. Wait 30 seconds for the model to load
2. If persists, restart the device
3. Check Settings → Info for available storage

### Generic or unhelpful answers
- Rephrase the question more specifically
- Include context about your situation
- Try breaking into smaller questions

### Image analysis not working
- Ensure adequate lighting in the photo
- Focus on the subject clearly
- Try a different angle

### "Cannot read sensors" message
- Check Settings → Sensors for sensor status
- Verify GPS has a fix (may take 1-2 minutes outdoors)
- Some sensors need calibration

## Privacy

- All conversations stay on your device
- No data is sent over the internet
- Clearing conversations permanently deletes them
- Your queries and photos are never used for AI training

## Summary

The AI Assistant is a powerful tool for outdoor adventures, but remember:

✅ **Use it for**: General information, sensor readings, quick reference

❌ **Don't rely on it for**: Identifying edible plants, medical diagnoses, critical navigation

🆘 **In emergencies**: Use SOS, not AI
