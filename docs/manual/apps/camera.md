# Camera

> Capture photos for documentation and AI analysis

## Overview

The Camera app captures photos that can be:
- Saved to your gallery
- Analyzed by the AI for plant/animal identification
- Attached to notes for context

## Getting Started

1. From Home, tap **Camera** (📷)
2. Point at your subject
3. Tap the **shutter button** to capture
4. Photo saves automatically to the gallery

## Taking Photos

### Basic Capture
1. Frame your subject in the viewfinder
2. Tap the large circle button to capture
3. A preview appears briefly
4. Photo is saved to gallery

### Flash
- Tap the **⚡** icon to toggle flash
- Options: Auto, On, Off

### Switching Cameras
- Tap the **🔄** icon to switch between front and rear cameras

## Photo Quality

Photos are optimized for:
- Field documentation
- AI image analysis
- Minimal storage usage

**Resolution**: Photos are captured at a resolution suitable for identification while conserving storage.

## Using Photos with AI

To identify plants, animals, or terrain:

1. Take a clear photo
2. Open the **AI** app
3. Tap **📷** and select from gallery (or take new photo)
4. Ask: "What is this plant?" or similar
5. AI will analyze and respond

### Tips for AI Analysis
- Get close to the subject
- Ensure good lighting
- Include distinctive features (leaves, bark, tracks)
- Take multiple angles if unsure

## Storage

Photos are stored locally on your device:
- Location: `/photos/` directory
- Format: JPEG
- Each photo is timestamped

Check storage usage:
1. Go to **Settings → Info**
2. View "Photos" in storage breakdown

## Troubleshooting

### Camera won't start
- Close and reopen the app
- Check if another app is using the camera
- Restart device if issue persists

### Photos are blurry
- Hold device steady
- Tap to focus before shooting
- Ensure adequate lighting
- Clean the camera lens

### Photos not saving
- Check available storage in Settings → Info
- Ensure the app has write permissions
- Restart the app

### Flash not working
- Check flash setting (may be on "Off")
- Verify flash hardware in Settings → Info
- Some devices may not have flash
