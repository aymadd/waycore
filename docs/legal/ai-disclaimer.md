## 5. Plant and Food Identification

⚠️ **DANGER: MISIDENTIFICATION CAN BE FATAL**

The AI's identification of plants, mushrooms, berries, or other wild foods may be **INCORRECT**.

- Many poisonous species closely resemble edible ones
- Photographs may not show distinguishing features
- Regional variations exist that the AI may not recognize
- Preparation methods matter for some species

**NEVER consume any wild plant, mushroom, or food based solely on AI identification.**

You MUST:
- Consult multiple expert field guides
- Seek verification from local experts
- When in doubt, do not consume
- Be aware that some species are fatal even in small amounts
